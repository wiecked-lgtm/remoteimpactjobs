About Remote Impact Jobs

I'm Stephanie, a marketing and communications professional with more than 15 years of experience at NGOs, cultural institutions and start-ups. I started this newsletter because most remote job boards are built for the US, and good impact roles in Europe are hard to find.

Which jobs are included

I only list paid roles in marketing and communications at organisations working on climate, policy, culture, education or tech for good. Each role must be doable from at least one European country. I check the original listing for the deadline and remote status, but things change quickly, so please confirm the details before you apply.

What ‘remote in Europe’ means

All jobs can be carried out remotely from at least one European country. Many are restricted to specific countries; this is always stated in the job description. For jobs that require you to be authorised to live or work in the UK, the description will state ‘UK work permit required’.
